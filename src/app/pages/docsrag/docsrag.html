DocsRAG

A production-minded retrieval-augmented-generation service: grounded, inline-cited answers over a documentation corpus. Built phase by phase with a defend-every-decision mindset — not a notebook demo.

§1Problem

Confident answers aren't the same as correct ones

LLMs answer confidently even when they’re wrong. For questions over a specific body of docs, that’s unacceptable — you need answers that are grounded in the source and traceable back to it. DocsRAG retrieves the relevant chunks first, then makes the model answer only from them, citing each claim. When the corpus doesn’t contain the answer, it declines rather than hallucinates.
